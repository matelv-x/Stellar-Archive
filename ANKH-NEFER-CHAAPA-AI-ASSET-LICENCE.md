# Ankh Nefer Chaapa'ai asset permission

The following Stellar Archive asset is derived from a landscape photograph
supplied by its owner:

- `payload/stellar-archive/assets/archive-approved-fan-096-ankh-nefer-chaapa-ai-v2.png`

On 19 November 2026, the contributor known as Jan confirmed that he owns the
original landscape photograph and permits the derived concept image to be
included in and distributed with the public Stellar Archive project.

The image is an artistic reconstruction. It is not a photograph of the
physical Stargate build and is not relicensed under the repository's MIT
software licence.
